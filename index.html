How it works 玩法

Two steps, in two separate transactions. Burning costs nothing but the NFTs themselves. Paying happens only when you mint — the classic whitelist-mint pattern.

STEP 1 — BURN

Burn 5 old NFTs → 1 qualification

5 eligible old NFTs → 🔥 → 1 qualification
  • Pick any 5 NFTs from the snapshot whitelist (Section 7 rules — only pre-snapshot tokenIds count).
  • All 5 are destroyed in one atomic transaction — native burn() where supported, otherwise sent to the public burn address 0x…dEaD where anyone can verify. If any step fails, the whole transaction reverts and you keep everything.
  • Your wallet is credited 1 qualification in the contract ledger. Burn 50 → 10 qualifications.
  • Per-wallet cap: 10 qualifications per season — filling all 5,000 slots needs at least 500 wallets.
STEP 2 — MINT

1 qualification + 1 USDT → new NFT + FORGE

1 qualification + 1 USDT → 1 blind-box NFT + 10,000 FORGE
  • Pay 1 USDT — the money enters the season contract in the same transaction, publicly visible.
  • You receive 1 brand-new Season 1 NFT (blind box). Rarity is rolled from the on-chain blockhash scheme described under Fairness — the result doesn't exist yet when you pay, and nobody can rig it.
  • You also receive 10,000 FORGE immediately. FORGE is transfer-locked until the pool opens, then unlocks automatically.
  • Max 10 mints per wallet per season — at most 0.2% of the season.

⚠ Read this before you burn

  • Burning is irreversible. Burned NFTs never come back and are never re-issued. Double-check your selection.
  • Qualifications are non-transferable — they live in the contract ledger, bound to your wallet. They cannot be sold or moved.
  • Qualifications expire at season end. If you burn and don't mint, the qualification is void and the burned NFTs are not refunded. Only burn if you actually intend to mint.

Where the money sits

  • During the sale, the project share (70%) is bookkept inside the contract only. There is no withdrawal function — nobody, including the owner, can touch it mid-sale.
  • At the moment the pool opens, the project share is released to the project treasury in that same transaction. Fully ours, no vesting on the cash.
  • The pool share (30%) accumulates as the USDT side of the opening pair.

If it doesn't sell out — the anti-bind clause

  • When the 10-day window ends below 5,000, the pool still opens automatically at the actual minted count, and FORGE unlocks. No manual step, no announcement to wait for.
  • All token allocations scale pro-rata with actual mints, so the opening reference price (≈0.00025 USDT) and the per-mint airdrop value (≈2.5 USDT at reference) are the same at any fill level — e.g. at 60% fill the project releases 2,100 USDT, not 3,500.
  • Un-issued airdrop tokens are burned at launch. There is no "70 short of the goal, everything locked forever" scenario — that failure mode is designed out.

At sellout, mint #5,000 triggers the launch in its own transaction: 6M FORGE + ≈1,500 USDT create the PancakeSwap pool (reference FDV ≈ 25,000 USDT), transfers unlock, and the LP is locked for 365 days. After launch, the 30% bucket's role converts to buybacks of floor Season-1 NFTs, which are then burned.

Fairness & safety 公平与安全

Everything below is a contract rule, not a promise. The source code is public and verified — check us.

🔑 .ai Agent ID only

Participation is gated at the contract level by the real .ai Agent ID registry (0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F). Only Binance Agentic Wallets holding an Agent ID can burn or mint. Per-wallet caps (10 qualifications / 10 mints) make cornering the season expensive and slow.

📸 Snapshot rule — no farming fresh mints

The biggest cheat in burn-to-mint is minting fresh free garbage just to burn it. So eligibility is frozen at a snapshot: only tokenIds that already existed at the snapshot block can be burned — for each whitelisted contract, ids up to its supply at that block. Anything minted after the snapshot, even from the same contract, doesn't count. Buying pre-snapshot stock on the secondary market to top up is allowed — that demand for dead floors is the point.

Snapshot block: 130,844,588 · Whitelisted contracts: 494 · Burnable stock covered: 1,267,418

🎲 Blockhash randomness — verifiable, honestly rated

When you subscribe, the contract locks commitBlock = current block + 3 — a block whose hash does not exist yet, so neither you nor we can know or influence the result. After that block is produced, anyone can settle: the seed is keccak256(blockhash(commitBlock), your address, mint index), exposed via the public pendingSeed() view for independent recomputation. If a draw isn't settled within the 256-block blockhash window, anyone may refresh the commit for free.

Honest note: blockhash randomness is weaker than Chainlink VRF — validators have a theoretical manipulation surface. We chose it deliberately to keep Season 1's operating cost at zero, and we say so here rather than dressing it up. The odds and the full derivation are public either way.

🚪 No backdoors

  • No withdraw / sweep / rescue functions. Sale funds cannot be drained mid-sale by anyone — the code paths simply don't exist.
  • Parameters freeze at season start: whitelist, price, airdrop size and odds cannot be changed mid-season by the admin.
  • Season launch is automatic (sellout or window end) — the team cannot delay it, front-run it, or quietly cancel.
  • Source verified: all 6 contracts are Sourcify exact_match — the deployed bytecode matches the published source byte for byte. Audit the claims yourself in the Contracts section.

Risks 风险

Stated plainly, because a project that hides its risks is telling you something.

  • Thin pool, violent swings. The opening pool is small by design (≈1,500 USDT + 6M FORGE at sellout, less if unfilled). Expect extreme volatility in both directions. The reference price ≈0.00025 USDT is arithmetic, not a price floor, target, or promise.
  • No return promises. Neither the NFT nor FORGE is an investment product. The airdrop's ≈2.5 USDT "value" is a reference-price calculation; the market may price it far lower — including near zero.
  • Blockhash randomness is weaker than VRF. Validators have a theoretical influence surface over blockhashes. Odds and derivation are public and recomputable, but the manipulation resistance is objectively below Chainlink VRF.
  • Burns are permanent. NFTs you burn are gone forever — including by mistake. Qualifications expire unused at season end with no refund.
  • Season 1 is an experiment. First live season of a new protocol: parameters (1 USDT, 5:1, 5,000 cap) are untested at market scale, NFT secondary royalties (5%, ERC-2981) depend on marketplace enforcement, and later seasons are plans, not commitments.
  • Smart-contract risk. Source is public and Sourcify-verified, and the design removes owner withdrawal paths — but no audit can make any contract risk-free. Only participate with funds and NFTs you can afford to lose entirely.

FAQ 问答

Can I transfer or sell my qualification?

No. Qualifications are an in-contract ledger entry bound to the wallet that burned. They are non-transferable by design — this is what stops qualification farming and resale markets from forming around them.

I burned the wrong NFT — can I get it back?

No. Burns are permanent and irreversible: there is no undo, no admin recovery, and no re-issue. This is a hard promise of the protocol, and it applies to mistakes too. Check your selection carefully before confirming — burned NFTs are never refunded, and an unused qualification simply expires at season end.

What happens if Season 1 doesn't sell out?

The season still completes. When the 10-day window ends, the pool opens automatically at the actual minted count, FORGE unlocks, and every allocation scales pro-rata — so the opening reference price (≈0.00025 USDT) and the ≈2.5 USDT reference value of each 10,000-FORGE airdrop hold at any fill level. Un-issued airdrop tokens are burned at launch. Nothing gets stuck.

When can I trade FORGE?

From the moment the pool opens — either in the transaction that mints #5,000 (sellout) or automatically when the window ends. Until that moment all FORGE transfers are locked for everyone, team included. The pool is on PancakeSwap (FORGE/USDT), with LP locked for 365 days.

Which NFTs can I burn?

Any ERC-721 from the season's whitelist — but only tokenIds that already existed at the snapshot block (snapshot block 130,844,588; 494 contracts, 1,267,418 burnable NFTs covered). NFTs minted after the snapshot don't count, even from a whitelisted contract. If you're short, buying pre-snapshot stock on the secondary market works — dust-floor NFTs are exactly the intended fuel.

When is Season 2?

After Season 1 concludes — the date will be announced once Season 1's results are in. Season-1 NFTs count as regular burn material next season (1 NFT = 1, no special multiplier), and Season-1 holders get early access to the next burn window. A 24M FORGE reserve is already set aside for future seasons' airdrops.
